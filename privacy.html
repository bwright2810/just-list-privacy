Just List Privacy Notice

Just List is a private tool operated by the seller for their own eBay account. It uses access granted through eBay OAuth to prepare, validate, and, when the seller approves, publish listings.

The tool sends listing information supplied by the seller, such as descriptions, photos, prices, and shipping settings, to eBay. The tool does not ask for the seller’s eBay password. Seller authorization is handled on eBay.

Application credentials are stored in encrypted local storage. The notification service holds the application credentials it needs to verify signed eBay requests in a restricted server file. It processes eBay account deletion notifications and logs delivery status without retaining notification payloads or user identifiers.

Just List does not collect buyer information for this listing workflow or sell personal information. The seller can revoke the app’s authorization in their eBay account settings and remove any locally saved tokens.
